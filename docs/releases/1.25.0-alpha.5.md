# Icod.Terminal 1.25.0-alpha.5

This prerelease gives large persistent Kitty raster transfers a bounded,
size-aware allowance beyond a control-sized query deadline. It
includes the alpha.4 Kitty DA1 correction and the earlier 1.25 prerelease fixes.

## Persistent raster transfer deadlines

Persistent resource uploads, animation-frame appends and partial-frame edits are
chunked Base64 transfers. Their query deadline previously remained fixed at one
second and began when the transaction entered the queue. Under WSL2/kitty, a
DCurses atlas could create its root resource and placeholder but time out while
uploading its second full-size animation frame.

Payload-bearing persistent transfers now receive a bounded allowance calculated
from their Base64 wire size at 512 KiB/s, followed by the existing one-second
acknowledgement window. The total remains capped at the query manager's existing
one-minute caller limit. Playback, frame selection and duration, composition,
placeholder, placement and other control-only transactions retain the one-second
deadline.

The change does not alter queue-time deadline semantics, response correlation,
late-response ownership, cancellation, output commitment, or conservative
resource and animation failure states. It introduces no public API.

## Compatibility and verification

Targets remain `net8.0`, `net9.0`, and `net10.0` on Windows, Linux and macOS.
Public API and production dependencies are unchanged. See
[Compatibility and Versioning](../Compatibility-and-Versioning.md).

Regression coverage uploads matching 1024x342 RGB24 root and animation frames,
waits for every chunk, advances the manual monotonic clock beyond the small
control deadline, and then completes only after the correlated Kitty `OK`.
The test-only candidate failed at the intended deadline assertion. The fixed
candidate passed the complete runtime and package qualification matrix.

## Subsequent live result and development hold

Alpha.5 has been merged and published. The downstream DCurses retest in Kitty
0.32.2 still times out on a missing chunked animation-upload acknowledgement and
falls back to ordinary FRAME. Input/display are correct, with command-driven
flicker. The bounded deadline does not cure the missing ACK.

On 2026-10-04 the maintainer put further graphics development and Kitty live
qualification on hold. No identifier workaround is added and persistent ATLAS
is not live accepted. See [the hold record](../Graphics-Development-Hold.md).
Stable 1.25 closure remains a separate decision.
